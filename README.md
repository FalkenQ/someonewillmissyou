# Someone Will Miss You

I made this website in the belief that mental health is something that needs to be spoken of more than it already is.
My thoughts are that mental health is something a lot of people struggle with, and that they struggle with how to reach out and how to talk about mental health difficulties.

## Description

This site is made for the purpose of helping people struggling with mental health, or someone who wanna reach out a helping hand.
For now people will be met by some words of encouragement, and a step by step on how you can get through this moment.

- Talk to someone will in the future lead to three different pages on this website, either the chat forum for some words of encouragement,
our chat forum for starting/creating a message and our page that takes you to your country's support line.
- In the hotline section you can choose your country or the country that you are currently in (limited to the Nordic countries right now).
Which will show you the support line, opening hours and emergency line.
- The purpose of the website and why mental health matters, and how to get through it together.
You can also contact us with either a question, site errors, ideas or something else that's on your mind.

## Planned features
- Messageboard, for people to leave a letter. That is either someone venting out, or someone typing in some words of encouragement.
The idea for that will be, you will be given a letter of encouragement at random to help you through your day.
- Also thinking about adding a check-in feature. With some additional features to it.

## Features
This website is for desktop and mobile use. Desktop version is planned with less scrolling. Plan was to make as much information fit on the screen without scrolling. Without making it feel cramped.
For the mobile version the navigation menu is hidden behind a collapsed mobile menu. For the mobile version, the items/texts will stay large for visual purpose. For those who might have a hard time reading on a smaller screen.
The website also uses JavaScript to validate the contact form and for the mobile navigation menu.
