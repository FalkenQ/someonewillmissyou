About:

Someone Will Miss You

Someone Will Miss You began with a simple belief: mental health deserves more attention. Behind a smile, someone may be facing struggles that others cannot see. We don’t always know what someone is going through, but we can make room to listen and care. This website was built because mental health matters. Through this project, we want to make it easier to find support, start conversations, and learn how to be there for someone who is struggling.

You don't have to face your struggles alone. Take the first step towards healing and support by reaching out to someone.
